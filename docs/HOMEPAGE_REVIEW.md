# Homepage review

Both variants retain the same assessment and coaching proposition, free allowance, prices and sign-in flow. Variant A leads with finding a development focus. Variant B leads with practical progress from a leadership challenge. They are copy previews rather than a statistically measured A/B experiment; there is no conversion tracking or automatic winner selection.

- A: https://leadership-skill-builder-staging-600309447137.europe-west1.run.app/?homepage=a
- B: https://leadership-skill-builder-staging-600309447137.europe-west1.run.app/?homepage=b

The free offer is one permanent scenario and practice in up to five assessment-derived micro skills, presented in focused sets. A prominent Start free button leads to account creation without a card. Paid cards show US$14.99 for one calendar month without renewal, US$9.99 monthly and US$99 annually, with renewal wording on each recurring option. Selecting a paid card leads to account creation; after sign-in the billing panel opens for review and checkout still requires an explicit click. Google sign-in is available in both account forms. Complimentary accounts do not enter checkout. Homepages do not expose organisation redemption or shared codes.

Staging is explicitly labelled as Stripe test mode. Production remains unchanged. Payment terms and formal privacy/retention review remain release tasks. Review the variants signed out or in a separate browser session; existing users normally see their practice dashboard.
